# Privacy Policy — Creepo

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Creepo ("the game"), developed by Karol Sheep Studios (Carolina S. Kirst), handles information when you play it on Android.

## Summary

Creepo does not collect, store or share any personal data on external servers. Everything the game needs is saved only on your own device.

## Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves the following data locally on your device:

- Game progress (the creature's state, day, growth stage, and settings such as language).
- Text you type when talking to the creature, if this feature is available in your version of the game. It is used only inside the game to shape the creature's responses and story.
- Information from your device's clock, and, where used, battery level, motion sensors and screen orientation. This is read only to drive in-game events and is not stored beyond what the game needs.

This data never leaves your device, and the developer has no access to it.

## Information we do not collect

The game does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

## Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information as part of providing the store and its services, as described in Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

Creepo is a psychological horror game intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

## Changes to this policy

If the game adds features that handle data differently (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the date above will change.
